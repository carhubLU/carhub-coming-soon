About CarHub

CarHub is being built to make vehicle services easier to find, compare and book.

The platform is designed for drivers who need services such as garages, tyre changes, detailing, car washes, paint protection film and other vehicle care solutions.

CarHub will also help users discover car events and stay connected to the automotive scene across Luxembourg and the Greater Region.

Our goal is to create one simple platform for vehicle care, bookings and car-related discovery across mobile and web.

CarHub is currently in development and will be launching soon.

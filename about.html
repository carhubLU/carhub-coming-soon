About

A simpler way to connect drivers with vehicle services.

CarHub is being built to help drivers find, compare and book vehicle-related services more easily.

The platform is designed for services such as garages, tyre changes, detailing, car washes, paint protection film and other vehicle care solutions.

CarHub will also help users discover car events and stay connected to the automotive scene in Luxembourg.

The goal is to create one simple platform for vehicle care, bookings and car-related discovery across mobile and web.

CarHub is currently in development and will be launching soon.
